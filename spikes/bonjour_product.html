Description

Dr. Althea Melaclear Cream (20g) is a clinically inspired, concentrated Korean skincare treatment formulated specifically to target stubborn hyperpigmentation, dark spots, and post-acne marks. 
Key Active Ingredients:
    • Tranexamic Acid (30,000 ppm): Interrupts melanin production pathways to reduce discoloration.
    • Niacinamide (30,000 ppm): Evens skin tone, strengthens the skin barrier, and minimizes pores.
    • Licorice Root Water (300,000 ppm): Delivers powerful anti-inflammatory and soothing properties.
    • Alpha-Arbutin: Promotes a more uniform and radiant complexion.
    • Ceramide NP & Hyaluronic Acid: Lock in deep hydration and reinforce skin health. 

Main Skin Benefits:
  • Fades acne marks, sun spots, and melasma.
  • Brightens dull skin and corrects uneven skin tones.
  • Soothes redness and skin sensitivity.
  • Deeply hydrates without leaving a heavy or greasy residue
